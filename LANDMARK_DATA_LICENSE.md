# Landmark coordinate attribution

`seoul-landmark-points.json` contains individually attributed location anchors.
Most anchors are derived from © OpenStreetMap contributors and subject to the
[Open Database License (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Those records identify their OpenStreetMap source in `source` and `sourceUrl`.
Two anchors use Seoul Metropolitan Government or Korea Tourism Organization
coordinates and identify the corresponding source page per record. The public
site displays all three sources. This notice covers the landmark-coordinate
data, not the restaurant listings or application code.
